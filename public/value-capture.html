Notes on the AI Frontier deck · 23 Oct 2026

Value goes to scarcity that does not leak.

An interactive reading of "가치는 어디로 가는가" for the US AI ecosystem map. Click through each part. The deck says there are only two kinds of lasting scarcity.

Physical bottlenecksPower, land, HBM, packaging, chips.Pays during the build-out.
Operation signalsOutcomes that only show up in real life. Did they buy again? Did the repair hold 90 days?Pays after the build-out.

Everything else leaks. Recipes spread in months. Data gets resold in about a year. Open models trail the frontier by about 4 months, and by about 6 weeks in coding. The same capability gets about 10x cheaper each year.

4 · Margin migration

As tokens get cheaper, who gains?

Token prices fall about 10x a year. Whoever prices per outcome, not per token, keeps that drop as margin. Bars show the deck's direction of change, not measured shares.

Known figures: chip gross margin about 75% today, lab inference margin about 70% (SemiAnalysis). Bar lengths for 2030 are illustrative of the deck's claims.

Read with care
  • This is one person's thesis from a Korean VC-style podcast. The author says his odds rest on thin data.
  • Many numbers come from one fast news week (Reuters, FT, ORTEX, UBS, SemiAnalysis). Check each against its original source before it goes on the map.
  • The deck is about finance and big players. It says little about early-stage startups, so the YC data still has to fill that gap.
